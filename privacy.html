Flutter Path – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 November 2026

Flutter Path ("the game") is made by an independent developer based in Çanakkale, Türkiye ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. The game has no accounts: we never ask for your name, email address or any other contact details.

1. Information stored only on your device

Your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, hidden timer, reminders), your progress through the levels, the map and the story, your coins and hints, your butterflies and showcase, your wardrobe items, your Daily Puzzle streaks and calendar, the level you are playing and your purchases are saved on your device. This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded videos (for example a free hint or double coins) and occasional full-screen ads between levels. "Remove Ads" and Flutter Club turn off the ads between levels; rewarded videos stay optional.

To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA), IP address, approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws, a consent message from Google's User Messaging Platform is shown before ads are requested; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. On iOS, the App Tracking Transparency prompt asks for permission before the IDFA is used. If you decline, ads are not personalized with it. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. Learn how Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Analytics (Google Firebase)

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is played and to tune its difficulty. It collects anonymous gameplay events (for example levels started, completed or left and how far you got, time spent, hints, undos and resets used, daily puzzles, mini games, shop visits, ads watched and purchases made), which screens are opened, the ad-frequency test group you are in, an app instance identifier, device model, operating system version and app version. This data does not identify you by name and is used only to improve the game. See firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

4. Purchases

Optional purchases ("Remove Ads", the Starter Pack, bundles of coins, hints and streak shields, the Garden Pass, Pip's outfit sets and the Flutter Club subscription) are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. We use RevenueCat to confirm purchases, keep Flutter Club active while your subscription runs and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, a random app user ID it creates, and device and app information. See revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

If you allow them, reminders that a new Daily Puzzle is ready are scheduled on your device as local notifications, at most one a day and never at night. Nothing is sent from a server. You can turn them off in Settings or in your device settings.

6. Sharing

The Share button copies a short text with your Daily Puzzle result (puzzle number, time and emoji) to your clipboard. It is only shared if you paste it somewhere yourself.

7. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

8. Data retention and your rights

Analytics data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye's KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to its processing. Because we do not know who you are, please include your device's app instance ID or advertising ID if you contact us, and we will forward deletion requests to Google and RevenueCat.

9. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.

10. Contact
